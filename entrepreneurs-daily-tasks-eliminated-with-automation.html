Daily Tasks Entrepreneurs Can Eliminate or Reduce

Automate repeatable rules, not ambiguous judgment. Keep approvals for money movement, legal commitments, personnel decisions, customer promises, and other high-consequence actions.

  • Scheduling coordination: booking links, reminders, and timezone handling.
  • Routine data movement: copying approved fields between forms, spreadsheets, and CRMs.
  • Standard follow-ups: reminders triggered by explicit status changes.
  • Reporting preparation: assembling dashboards and recurring summaries.
  • File and inbox routing: labeling, categorizing, and assigning routine items.
