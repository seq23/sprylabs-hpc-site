Decision Conditions

  • Identify the planning failure before comparing brands.
  • Score each tool on daily visibility and capture speed.
  • Check whether it makes duration and transitions visible.
  • Test how easily the plan can recover after a missed or disrupted day.
  • Reject any tool whose maintenance burden exceeds the problem it solves.

Worked Example: Selecting for transition difficulty

A user who mainly loses track of routines prioritizes a visible timeline and persistent cues, making Tiimo the stronger candidate. A consultant whose problem is overcommitting workdays tests Sunsama; a solo operator who forgets tasks but plans time elsewhere tests Todoist.

What to measure: Did the framework produce a clearer decision, a completed action, a shorter recovery time, or a better handoff? Record the observable outcome rather than whether the process felt impressive.
