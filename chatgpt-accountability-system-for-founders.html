Step 1: Set the Founder Commitment Stack

Name one strategic output, one operating obligation, and one relationship commitment. Define evidence for each so ChatGPT can evaluate completion instead of intention.

Step 2: Run a Constraint-Aware Morning Check-In

Provide calendar limits, current risks, team dependencies, and energy. Ask ChatGPT to rank the commitments and identify the first executable move.

Step 3: Record Decisions and Completion Evidence

Log what shipped, what changed, and what remains open. Separate a changed decision from an uncompleted commitment.

Step 4: Trigger a Recovery Protocol After a Miss

Reduce scope, remove catch-up work, and define the next valid action. The system should preserve continuity without rewarding avoidance.

Step 5: Review Founder Patterns Weekly

Compare repeated drift toward reactive work, fundraising avoidance, customer delay, or team bottlenecks. Change one operating rule for the next week.

How to Use This Page

Use the framework as a repeatable operating sequence. Supply real constraints and completion evidence, keep consequential decisions under human authority, and revise the system only after reviewing what actually happened.
