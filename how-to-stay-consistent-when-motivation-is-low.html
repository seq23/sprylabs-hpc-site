Consistency when motivation is low comes from lowering the floor, removing catch-up pressure, and closing one visible loop instead of waiting to feel ready.

The core rule

Do not use motivation as the starting condition. Use a minimum viable day: one bounded action, one completion signal, and no self-punishment for reduced scope.

The low-motivation protocol

  1. Name the smallest action that still counts.
  2. Cap it at 10 to 25 minutes.
  3. Remove optional tasks from the day.
  4. Report DONE when the loop closes.
  5. Do not add catch-up work afterward.

Why this beats streak pressure

Streak pressure makes one miss feel fatal. Continuity architecture treats one miss as data and protects the next start.
