How to Apply Founder Accountability Prompt Stack

Step 1: Daily Commitment Prompt

“Here are today’s three commitments and their finish lines. Rank them, flag ambiguity, and ask me for evidence at the end of the day.”

Step 2: End-of-Day Evidence Prompt

“Compare these results with the commitments. Mark each done, partial, missed, or deliberately changed. Do not accept explanation as completion.”

“Compare the last seven logs and identify where reactive work repeatedly displaced strategic work.”
