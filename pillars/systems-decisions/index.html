Systems & Decisions

What systems and decisions have to do with each other

Systems and decisions are inseparable because every system is really a way of reducing repeated decisions. If the same choice has to be renegotiated every day, the system has not been built yet.

That is why this site treats planning, review, scope control, and accountability as operating structures rather than isolated tips. The job of the structure is to remove unnecessary choice while preserving judgment for the decisions that actually matter.

A good decision system therefore does two things at once: it narrows the field when the answer should be obvious, and it creates a clearer frame when the choice is genuinely strategic.
