What leverage means here

Leverage means building moves that keep working after the moment you make them. That includes decision rules, reusable assets, systems, distribution, delegation, and frameworks that reduce repeat effort. On this site, leverage is the opposite of heroic overexertion.

    Why leverage matters operationally

    Leverage matters because time, attention, and willpower are all scarce. A good leverage system preserves decision quality by turning repeated work into reusable assets, cleaner defaults, or calmer rules.

    That is why this pillar connects execution to writing, systems, distribution, delegation, and compounding advantage. The point is not doing more. The point is making the right work keep paying after the first effort.
