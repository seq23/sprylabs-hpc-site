Burnout & Recovery

What burnout means in this system

Burnout here does not mean mild tiredness. It means the operating system is overloaded enough that even obvious tasks start to feel heavy, delayed, or emotionally expensive.

That is why recovery is treated as a systems problem first. If the day is too wide, the standards are too brittle, or the loop keeps reopening unfinished commitments, the answer is not more force. The answer is containment, clearer floors, and fewer active decisions.

A useful recovery page should therefore translate stress into operational moves: shrink scope, reduce active obligations, define one stabilizing output, and protect tomorrow from today’s chaos.
