Accountability

How accountability is defined on this site

Accountability on this site does not mean public pressure or streak theater. It means closing the loop between the plan, the action, and the review so the next day starts clean.

That matters because most accountability systems fail when they rely on emotion instead of procedure. A usable system needs visible rules, realistic floors, and a stable way to report what happened without spiraling into self-judgment.

The practical version is simple: define the day, constrain scope, run the work, then use a short check-in to mark what is done, what moved, and what carries forward. Accountability becomes boring on purpose, which is exactly why it survives pressure.
