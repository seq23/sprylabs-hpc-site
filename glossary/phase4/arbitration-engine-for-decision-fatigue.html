Five-dimension arbitration grid

DimensionWhat it asksWhy it is on the grid
LeverageDoes finishing this make other work cheaper or unnecessary?High-leverage work is systematically under-chosen because its payoff is indirect.
UrgencyDoes a real external deadline exist, or an internal one?Separating the two stops manufactured urgency from winning every tie.
Energy fitDoes this match the capacity actually available in this block?A correct choice at the wrong energy level produces a stalled block.
CompoundingDoes this get easier or more valuable if repeated?Compounding work loses to urgent work every day unless it is scored.
