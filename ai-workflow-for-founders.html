Purpose: convert obligations and constraints into a realistic day. Founder input: calendar, deadlines, dependencies, and current energy. ChatGPT action: organize the inputs.

Output: a bounded daily map.

Phase 2: Prioritize

Purpose: select the work that changes the business. Founder input: revenue, customer, team, investor, and product commitments. ChatGPT action: compare leverage, urgency, and downside.

Output: one foreground priority and a short maintenance list.

Phase 3: Execute

Purpose: begin before planning expands. Founder input: the first deliverable and available time. ChatGPT action: reduce ambiguity and define the first physical action.

Output: a time-boxed execution block.

Phase 4: Review

Purpose: close loops and improve tomorrow. Founder input: completed, partial, and missed outputs. ChatGPT action: identify patterns without shame.

Output: the next step, one system adjustment, and a clean close.
