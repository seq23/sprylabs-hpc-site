Why people struggle

Founders and operators often use AI in fragmented ways: drafting here, brainstorming there, but without a stable operating loop that actually reduces mental load. The result is more motion without more control.

What actually works

What works better is treating the model as part of a weekly operating system for planning, review, prioritization, and decision prep. The workflow needs to be bounded enough to survive a busy week.
