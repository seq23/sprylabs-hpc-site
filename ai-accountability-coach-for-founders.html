What Accountability Means for a Founder

  • Protecting the one strategic output that reactive work keeps displacing.
  • Closing commitments made to customers, investors, and the team.
  • Detecting when planning becomes avoidance.
  • Restarting after a miss without rebuilding the entire operating system.
