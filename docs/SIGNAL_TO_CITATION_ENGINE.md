# Social Signals → Citation Pages Engine

Purpose: collect broadly, score tightly, publish selectively. The audience is people who want discipline, life-coach/accountability structure, and a way to juggle multiple projects/facets of life: professionals, athletes, parents/caregivers, entrepreneurs, and high-agency operators.

Rules:
- Reddit/social intake must be throttled and warning-only when a source fails.
- High-intent extraction comes before publishing.
- Signals must show discipline, accountability, planning, decision-fatigue, burnout, multi-project, or coaching/product intent.
- Low-intent social noise is discarded before page generation.
- White papers release at least twice per year through the scheduled queue.
