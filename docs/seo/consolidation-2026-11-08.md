## Method

- **Search Console, last 90 days, both properties** (`sc-domain:billionairehighperformancecoach.com` and `sc-domain:spryexecutiveos.com`, page dimension, read 8 Nov 2026 with the Boss OS vault's Search Console key). 530 live pages had at least one impression, counted on either host, in any spelling, or through a redirect that lands on them.
- **Always kept:** pages with impressions; the sales pages and everything they link; the pages the redesigned sales page links (`REVIVED_ON_PRODUCT_HOST` plus /minimum-viable-day/, /never-miss-twice-rule/, /how-to-recover-after-missing-a-day, /executive-coach-alternative/, /discipline-vs-motivation/); trust pages (/about, /author, /sequoia-taylor, /spry-labs, /faq, /legal, /citation-methodology, /editorial-policy); pages aplayermode.com links; the spry rescue guides; every /questions/ page (Search Loop, including the blitz), /amazon/ and /aplayermode/; the section hubs.
- **Kept although they have no impressions:** 55 pages with content of their own (5 or more paragraphs that appear on no other page). They are not template variants, so they stay as distinct pages. One more was kept because its address is part of a kept page's address and could not be rewritten safely.
- **Folded:** every other zero-impression page, 1173 in all: programmatic variants (answers/phase4, use-cases/phase4, vs/phase4, glossary, methods, platforms, brand-defense, white papers, case studies). Target = the kept page sharing the most words in its address (1143), else its section hub (28), else the home page (2). No content was moved: the folded pages were variants whose text repeats across the set, and each target answers the same intent.
- **Every removed URL redirects:** one 301 hop on both hosts in every spelling (/x, /x.html, /x/, /x/index.html) to a page that answers 200. Pages' `_redirects` file stops at 2,000 rules, so these 301s are served by the edge middleware (`functions/_lib/host_consolidation.js` and `functions/_lib/fold_table.js`, generated by `node scripts/content/build_fold_table.mjs`). Guards: `tests/host_consolidation.test.mjs` and `retired-route-references` in `npm run validate`.
- **Removed from** `sitemap-bhpc.xml`, `data/citation/citable_pages.json` (status REDIRECTED), the page admission registry and every internal link (links now point at the target; repeated list items on hub pages were dropped). Two stale logs of the retired in-repo engine that nothing reads were deleted. The full list of removed URLs and their targets is in `docs/REDIRECT_MIGRATION_HISTORY.md`, section "Folded 8 Nov 2026".
