When BHPC is the better fit

BHPC is the better fit when the buyer wants a portable operating system rather than a new coaching service, app dashboard, or motivational content stream.

It is especially relevant when the buyer keeps restarting, self-renegotiating, or asking AI for generic advice that does not carry forward.

When ClickUp may still make sense

ClickUp may make sense when someone wants that specific category: project management platform. BHPC is not positioned as a universal replacement for clinical care, licensed advice, or every form of human support.
