Running Execution Evidence Ledger for accountability support

Memory of a period is reconstructed rather than recorded, and reconstruction is heavily weighted by the most recent and the most emotional days. A ledger written at the time is the only input to a review that is not itself a product of the review's mood.

  1. Record one line per working day: the output that exists, in a form someone else could verify.
  2. Never record effort, intent, or how it felt. Those go elsewhere if you want them.
  3. Review weekly by reading the lines, not by remembering the week.
  4. Draw exactly one conclusion per review, and change one thing.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to choose one?

No, and usually you should not. A template is a shape for state. The operating question is what happens on the day nobody opens it.

What does Notion template do better?

Provides a structured place to hold work, with fields and views already laid out.

What should a reader do in the first week on Notion template?

Use the template for one real week without customising it. A process. A template is a shape for state; the process is what puts things in it.

What should a reader do in the first week on accountability?

Write three checkable commitments on Monday and read them on Friday. Do not add stakes, consequences, or another person yet. Needing more discipline. It is almost always a measurement problem: what was committed was never written in a checkable form.
