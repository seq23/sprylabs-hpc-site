Running Arbitration Engine for priority selection support

Ties do not break by thinking harder, because the two options are close on the dimension you are thinking about. Scoring them on five named dimensions moves the comparison somewhere the difference is visible, and forces the trade to be stated rather than felt.

  1. Name the competing options explicitly, at most three. More than three is not arbitration, it is an unmade decision.
  2. Score each on leverage, urgency, energy fit, compounding, and downside if skipped.
  3. Say which dimension you are letting decide, before you look at the totals.
  4. Write the losing option's next review date so it is parked rather than abandoned.
