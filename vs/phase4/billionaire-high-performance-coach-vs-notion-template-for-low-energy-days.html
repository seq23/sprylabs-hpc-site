Running Continuity Over Intensity Rule for low-energy days support

Effort is elastic and schedules are not. A commitment sized to a good week fails in a normal one, and each failure costs more than the extra output of the good week bought. Sizing to the worst plausible week produces a commitment that survives, and surviving is what accumulates.

  1. Describe your worst plausible week honestly: illness, travel, a crisis at work.
  2. Size the commitment so it is still doable in that week, not in this one.
  3. Write the floor version explicitly, so that on a bad day there is something to do rather than a decision to make.
  4. Let good weeks overflow, but never let the overflow raise the floor.
