Running No Mid-Day Renegotiation Rule for strategic focus support

The morning plan and the afternoon rewrite are made by the same person in different states. The afternoon state has more fatigue and less context about why the morning chose what it chose, so its rewrites systematically favor the easier item. Freezing the plan does not make the afternoon smarter; it stops the afternoon from deciding.

  1. Fix the plan in the morning and treat it as closed to changes from that point.
  2. When the urge to rewrite arrives, write the proposed change down instead of making it.
  3. Finish the current block anyway. The urge usually arrives at the hardest point of the block, not at a neutral moment.
  4. Review the written proposals at the end of the day and let tomorrow's plan absorb the ones that still make sense.
