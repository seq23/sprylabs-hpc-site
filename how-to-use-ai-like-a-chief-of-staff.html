An AI chief of staff is a language model set up to do what a chief of staff does for an executive: keep the priority list, prepare briefs before meetings, track decisions and owners, and run a weekly review of what is slipping. It needs your calendar context and explicit rules; without them it is only a note-taker.

What can an AI chief of staff tool do?

Draft the daily priority brief, prepare one-page notes before each meeting, turn meeting notes into decisions with owners and dates, and flag overdue commitments at the weekly review.

What does a human chief of staff do that AI cannot?

Represent you to people, read organisational politics and make judgment calls on your behalf. AI handles the information flow; a person handles the relationships.

How do you set up AI as a chief of staff?

Give it a standing brief: your role, top three priorities, decision rules and the format you want for briefs. Start each day with the priority brief and end each week with the slipping-commitments review.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best AI chief of staff tool?

Any capable LLM you already use, configured with a standing brief and fixed routines. The routines - daily brief, meeting prep, weekly review - matter more than which model runs them.

How can chiefs of staff use AI?

To draft briefs and agendas, summarise long threads, track decisions and owners and prepare the executive's weekly review, which frees time for the people work only a human can do.
