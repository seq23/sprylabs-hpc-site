Why people struggle

Consistency collapses when the system treats one missed day like a full reset. That creates shame, loss of momentum, and a cycle where every interruption feels bigger than it should.

What actually works

What works better is preserving continuity with a smaller execution floor and a clear re-entry rule. Systems should be designed to survive imperfect days instead of demanding perfection.
