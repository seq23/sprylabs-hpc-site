Accountability Mirror Protocol — a solo operator who is behind on commitments

You are the whole operation: delivery, admin, sales, and the switching between them.

The hardest part of the week: protecting a block long enough to finish delivery work before an admin item interrupts it.

Do this first. Write the commitment before the period starts, in a form that can be checked without interpretation.

Do not do this. Scoring effort. Effort is always available as an explanation, so any period can be scored as a success and the mirror stops reflecting anything.

There is a set of things other people are waiting on, and the waiting is itself now part of the load. Do not disappear until you can deliver. Silence costs more than a late delivery with a date attached.

When this framework is the right one. Use it when your sense of how the week went and what actually got finished have started to disagree.

  1. Write the commitment before the period starts, in a form that can be checked without interpretation.
  2. At the end of the period, list only what exists: files, sent messages, shipped changes, decisions recorded.
  3. Put the two lists side by side and mark each commitment met, partially met, or not met.
  4. Change one thing for the next period, and change the commitment rather than the effort.
