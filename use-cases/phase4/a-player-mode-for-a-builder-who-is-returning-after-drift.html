When this framework is the right one. Use it the morning after a day you did not do the thing, while the miss is still a single event and not yet a story about who you are.

  1. Write the miss down in one line with no explanation attached: date, what did not happen. Explanations are what turn a gap into a case.
  2. Do not schedule a catch-up. Yesterday's block is gone and reclaiming it is what makes today fail too.
  3. Name the smallest version of the same action that fits today, and put a time on it before you close the note.
  4. Do that version today even if it is visibly smaller than usual. The point of today is the streak of participation, not the size of the output.

What is hardest about this for a builder?

What should a builder not do while returning after drift?

Do not analyze the drift before restarting. The analysis becomes the project and the system stays stopped. Return at normal size or smaller. Never at larger.

What should a reader do in the first week on builder?

Spend the first block of every day on something that is not building, and record what it produced. Needing to build something better. Version four of an unused product is not a build problem.

What should a reader do in the first week on returning after drift?

Restart one component today and leave everything else stopped for the rest of the week. Needing to start over. Most of the system is intact; a rebuild discards the intact parts along with the broken one.
