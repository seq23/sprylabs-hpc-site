When this framework is the right one. Use it when an opportunity is in front of you and the question is whether it is worth entering at all.

  1. Ask what you would own at the end, not what you would be paid during.
  2. Ask whether the work is repeatable without you, or whether it stops the day you stop.
  3. Ask whether the payoff distribution is asymmetric: is there a version where this is large.
  4. Ask what the worst realistic outcome costs, and whether you can carry that cost without it ending other things.
