Rewarding vs Burnout Strategy Filter

The Rewarding vs Burnout Strategy Filter separates systems that compound energy, clarity, and self-trust from tactics that create short-term output while increasing recovery debt.

How to apply it

  1. Check whether the strategy lowers tomorrow’s decision load.
  2. Check whether it includes a realistic stop rule.
  3. Check whether it works on low-energy days.
  4. Check whether it creates evidence instead of shame.
  5. Keep strategies that compound capacity; cut strategies that require emergency intensity.
