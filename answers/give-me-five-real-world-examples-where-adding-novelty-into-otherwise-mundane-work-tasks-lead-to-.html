Novelty Injection Method

The Novelty Injection Method adds a small, controlled change to a repetitive task so attention re-engages without turning the task into entertainment or distraction.

How to apply it

  1. Answer email as a 20-minute scoreboard instead of an open inbox.
  2. Turn expense cleanup into a batch sprint with a visible remaining count.
  3. Do planning on paper first, then transfer only the final decisions.
  4. Take a walking call for low-stakes status updates.
  5. Use a different workspace for repetitive admin so the task has a fresh context.
