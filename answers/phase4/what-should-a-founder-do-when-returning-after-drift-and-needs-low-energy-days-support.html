Recovery Mode Operating Rule — low-energy days while returning after drift

The system stopped some time ago, the exact date is unclear, and returning feels like it requires an explanation.

What is actually scarce here: a starting point. Everything is equally stopped so there is no obvious first move.

Do this first. Restart one component today, chosen because the others depend on it, and leave everything else stopped for a week.

Do not do this. Do not analyze the drift before restarting. The analysis becomes the project and the system stays stopped.

What work is the right work when the state does not match the plan? Categorize work by energy profile in advance and take work from the matching category.

When this framework is the right one. Use it in a stretch of days where capacity is genuinely reduced and is not going to bounce back tomorrow.

  1. Declare the mode explicitly and give it an end date or a review date. An undeclared reduction is just a decline.
  2. Set the reduced standard in writing: what a completed day looks like while the mode is on.
  3. Suspend anything that only works at full capacity, by name, rather than letting it fail quietly.
  4. Review on the date. Either exit the mode or extend it deliberately, and if you extend twice, get outside input.
