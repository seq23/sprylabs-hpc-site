Foreground Priority Selection Rule — system drift while overwhelmed

Every item on the list feels equally urgent, which is the reliable sign that none of them is being assessed.

What is actually scarce here: attention, not time. The hours may exist; what is missing is the ability to hold one thing still long enough to work it.

Do this first. Take one item off the list, out loud, and work it for twenty-five minutes without looking at the rest.

Do not do this. Do not re-plan. Re-planning under overwhelm produces a longer list and a stronger sense of how much there is.

How did this stop running without anyone deciding to stop it? Audit each component against evidence rather than memory, and restart one at a time with a week between.

When this framework is the right one. Use it when several projects are all technically active and none of them is visibly moving.

  1. Name one project as foreground for a stated period, measured in weeks rather than days.
  2. Give every other active project an explicit background status with a maintenance floor: the minimum that keeps it from decaying.
  3. When a block is contested, the foreground project takes it. This is the entire rule and its whole value is that it is decided in advance.
  4. Review the foreground choice on a fixed date, not when a background project starts feeling urgent.

Worked example: a founder with three unowned items and one investor update due

It is 09:40. The investor update is due Friday, two hires are waiting on a decision that only they can make, and a customer email arrived that will take an hour to answer properly. Nothing on that list has an owner other than them. Every item on the list feels equally urgent, which is the reliable sign that none of them is being assessed.

Take one item off the list, out loud, and work it for twenty-five minutes without looking at the rest. Do not re-plan. Re-planning under overwhelm produces a longer list and a stronger sense of how much there is.

The system drift move is specific: audit each component against evidence rather than memory, and restart one at a time with a week between. The list is the same size and one item is smaller. That is the only reliable move from this state.

What to measure. The component audit and the restart record.

Running Foreground Priority Selection Rule for system drift support

Parallel projects do not share time evenly; they share it in whatever proportion the day's noise dictates, which usually means none of them gets a block long enough to produce a result. Naming one foreground project does not stop the others; it decides in advance which one wins a contested block.
