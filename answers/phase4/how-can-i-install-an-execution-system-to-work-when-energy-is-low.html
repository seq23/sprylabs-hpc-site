Work has an energy profile and so does a block. Mismatch produces a stalled hour, and the stall gets attributed to discipline rather than to the mismatch.

Do not do this. Forcing high-category work through a low block. It produces an unproductive hour and a conclusion about yourself, and neither is useful.

This level fits when: you have a design that has survived contact with at least one real day. It does not fit when: you are still redesigning. Installing an unsettled design means reinstalling repeatedly, which is how people conclude systems do not work for them.

When this framework is the right one. Use it when the block in front of you does not match the state you are actually in, and the choice looks like force it or lose it.

  1. Read your current state honestly and quickly: high, medium, or low. Do not negotiate with it.
  2. Categorize your work the same way, in advance, so the matching does not require a decision when you are already low.
  3. Take work from the matching category. A low block spent on low-category work is a productive block.
  4. Note mismatches. Repeated mismatch at the same hour is a scheduling fact, not a discipline fact.
