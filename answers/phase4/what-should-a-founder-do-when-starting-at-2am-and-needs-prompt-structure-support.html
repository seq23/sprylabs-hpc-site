When this framework is the right one. Use it in a stretch of days where capacity is genuinely reduced and is not going to bounce back tomorrow.

  1. Declare the mode explicitly and give it an end date or a review date. An undeclared reduction is just a decline.
  2. Set the reduced standard in writing: what a completed day looks like while the mode is on.
  3. Suspend anything that only works at full capacity, by name, rather than letting it fail quietly.
  4. Review on the date. Either exit the mode or extend it deliberately, and if you extend twice, get outside input.
