Continuity Over Intensity Rule — strategic focus while starting at 2am

The work is happening at hours that do not repeat, driven by whatever state made tonight possible.

What is actually scarce here: repeatability. Output produced in an unrepeatable state cannot be planned around.

Do this first. Capture what you produced, then set the smallest version of the same work at an hour that exists every day.

Do not do this. Do not build a system around the good night. It is sized for a state you cannot schedule.

How does the important work stop losing to the loud work? Name one foreground project for a period measured in weeks and give it every contested block by rule.

When this framework is the right one. Use it when planning a commitment you intend to keep for months, and the version you are about to commit to is the version you could do this week.

  1. Describe your worst plausible week honestly: illness, travel, a crisis at work.
  2. Size the commitment so it is still doable in that week, not in this one.
  3. Write the floor version explicitly, so that on a bad day there is something to do rather than a decision to make.
  4. Let good weeks overflow, but never let the overflow raise the floor.

Running Continuity Over Intensity Rule for strategic focus support
