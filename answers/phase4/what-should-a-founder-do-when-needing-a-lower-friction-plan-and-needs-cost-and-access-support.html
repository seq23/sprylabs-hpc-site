What is actually scarce here: tolerance for setup steps. Each one is cheap and their sum is what stops the plan.

Do this first. Walk the plan and count the steps between deciding to start and actually starting. Remove the largest one.

Do not do this. Do not add discipline. Friction is a design property and discipline is an expensive way to pay for it daily.

What does this actually require, and what happens if I cannot pay for the convenient version? Separate what the method needs from what a particular product offers, and keep the method in a form that works without the product.

When this framework is the right one. Use it the moment you notice you have read the same list item three times without starting it.

  1. Stop trying to start. The re-reading is the diagnosis, not a discipline failure.
  2. Ask what the first physical thing your hands would do. If there is no answer, the item is not a task.
  3. If a decision is hidden inside it, extract the decision and make that the item.
  4. Rewrite the item as the physical action, and leave the original phrasing next to it so the pattern is visible later.
