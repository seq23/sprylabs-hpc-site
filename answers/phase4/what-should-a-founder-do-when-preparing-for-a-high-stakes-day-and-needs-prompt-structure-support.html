When this framework is the right one. Use it on mornings where the first hour reliably disappears into inputs and the real work starts after lunch or not at all.

  1. Do one physical, non-screen action first. It exists to move you out of bed-state, not to be virtuous.
  2. Read one pre-written card that names today's first output. Deciding at 7am with no context is how the hour is lost.
  3. Work the first block on that output before opening any queue: mail, messages, feeds, tickets.
  4. Open the queues only after the block ends, and treat what is in them as input to the rest of the day rather than a re-plan.
