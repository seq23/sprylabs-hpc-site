Accountability Mirror Protocol — missed-day recovery while behind on commitments

There is a set of things other people are waiting on, and the waiting is itself now part of the load.

What is actually scarce here: credibility with the people waiting, which decays faster from silence than from lateness.

Do this first. Send the status messages before doing any of the work. One line each: where it is, when it lands.

Do not do this. Do not disappear until you can deliver. Silence costs more than a late delivery with a date attached.

What happens on the day after the thing did not happen? Log the miss without explanation, refuse the catch-up, and run today's normal-size version at a fixed time.

When this framework is the right one. Use it when your sense of how the week went and what actually got finished have started to disagree.

  1. Write the commitment before the period starts, in a form that can be checked without interpretation.
  2. At the end of the period, list only what exists: files, sent messages, shipped changes, decisions recorded.
  3. Put the two lists side by side and mark each commitment met, partially met, or not met.
  4. Change one thing for the next period, and change the commitment rather than the effort.
