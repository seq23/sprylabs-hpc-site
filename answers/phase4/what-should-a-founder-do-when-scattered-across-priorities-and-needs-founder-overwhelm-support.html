Arbitration Engine — founder overwhelm while scattered across priorities

Several projects are active, each has had some attention this week, and none has produced anything visible.

What is actually scarce here: contiguous blocks. Everything is getting minutes and nothing is getting an hour.

Do this first. Name one foreground project for a stated period and give the others a written maintenance floor.

Do not do this. Do not try to give each project a fair share. Fairness at the project level produces failure at every project.

When this framework is the right one. Use it when two or more things genuinely deserve the same block and the tie is not breaking on its own.

  1. Name the competing options explicitly, at most three. More than three is not arbitration, it is an unmade decision.
  2. Score each on leverage, urgency, energy fit, compounding, and downside if skipped.
  3. Say which dimension you are letting decide, before you look at the totals.
  4. Write the losing option's next review date so it is parked rather than abandoned.

Worked example: a founder with three unowned items and one investor update due
