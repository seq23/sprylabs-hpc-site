Executive Review Mode — motivation dependency while facing decision fatigue

What is actually scarce here: decision capacity for the day, most of which has gone to choices that recur and could have been defaults.

Do this first. Name the three decisions you have made today that you also made yesterday, and write a default for each.

Do not do this. Do not try to decide better. The problem is the count, not the quality.

What happens on the days the motivation is not there? Size the commitment against the worst plausible week and write the floor version explicitly.

When this framework is the right one. Use it when you already know most of what you need to know and the problem is that it is not organized into a decision.

  1. State the decision that is actually waiting, in one sentence ending in a verb.
  2. List only what you already know that bears on it. New research is out of scope for the session.
  3. Separate the list into facts, assumptions, and unknowns, and mark which unknowns would actually change the decision.
  4. Make the decision, or name the single piece of information that would settle it and how you will get it.
