The work is happening at hours that do not repeat, driven by whatever state made tonight possible.

What is actually scarce here: repeatability. Output produced in an unrepeatable state cannot be planned around.

Do this first. Capture what you produced, then set the smallest version of the same work at an hour that exists every day.

Do not do this. Do not build a system around the good night. It is sized for a state you cannot schedule.

How do I stop spending the day's judgment on choices that repeat? Convert recurring low-consequence choices into standing defaults with written override conditions.

When this framework is the right one. Use it when the block in front of you does not match the state you are actually in, and the choice looks like force it or lose it.

  1. Read your current state honestly and quickly: high, medium, or low. Do not negotiate with it.
  2. Categorize your work the same way, in advance, so the matching does not require a decision when you are already low.
  3. Take work from the matching category. A low block spent on low-category work is a productive block.
  4. Note mismatches. Repeated mismatch at the same hour is a scheduling fact, not a discipline fact.
