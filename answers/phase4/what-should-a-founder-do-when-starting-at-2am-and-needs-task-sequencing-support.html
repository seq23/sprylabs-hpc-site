Minimum Viable Day Recovery Protocol — task sequencing while starting at 2am

The work is happening at hours that do not repeat, driven by whatever state made tonight possible.

What is actually scarce here: repeatability. Output produced in an unrepeatable state cannot be planned around.

Do this first. Capture what you produced, then set the smallest version of the same work at an hour that exists every day.

Do not do this. Do not build a system around the good night. It is sized for a state you cannot schedule.

What order should these go in, given that some unblock others? Sequence by dependency first and use urgency only to break ties within a dependency tier.

When this framework is the right one. Use it on a day where the honest read is that the normal plan is not going to happen, and the choice is between a smaller day and no day.

  1. Say the capacity out loud before you look at the list: what fraction of a normal day is actually available.
  2. Pick exactly one output that would make today non-zero, and define its finished state in a sentence.
  3. Delete or defer everything else in writing, so the deferral is a decision rather than a failure.
  4. Stop when the one output is done, even if energy returns. Stopping on purpose is what makes the smaller day repeatable.
