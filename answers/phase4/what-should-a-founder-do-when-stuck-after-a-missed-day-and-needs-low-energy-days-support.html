Capacity-Matched Execution Rule — low-energy days while stuck after a missed day

Yesterday did not happen and today is being spent deciding what to do about yesterday.

What is actually scarce here: nothing, actually. The capacity is there and it is being spent on the accounting.

Do this first. Log the miss in one line with no explanation, then run today's normal-size action at a fixed time.

Do not do this. Do not schedule a catch-up and do not write a post-mortem on a single miss.

What work is the right work when the state does not match the plan? Categorize work by energy profile in advance and take work from the matching category.

When this framework is the right one. Use it when the block in front of you does not match the state you are actually in, and the choice looks like force it or lose it.

  1. Read your current state honestly and quickly: high, medium, or low. Do not negotiate with it.
  2. Categorize your work the same way, in advance, so the matching does not require a decision when you are already low.
  3. Take work from the matching category. A low block spent on low-category work is a productive block.
  4. Note mismatches. Repeated mismatch at the same hour is a scheduling fact, not a discipline fact.
