Copy-and-use prompt

Act as my weekly scheduling chief of staff. Build a realistic time-blocked week.

Fixed calendar: [PASTE]
Top 3 outcomes: [OUTCOMES]
Task list with rough durations: [TASKS]
High-energy windows: [TIMES]
Low-energy/admin windows: [TIMES]
Required buffers or personal constraints: [CONSTRAINTS]

Return:
1. A Monday-Friday calendar-ready block plan.
2. Deep-work blocks tied to the top outcomes.
3. Batched admin/communication blocks.
4. Transition and overflow buffers.
5. Tasks that do not fit and what to defer/delegate/delete.
6. A capacity verdict: realistic or overbooked.
Never schedule overlapping work or invent open time.

Implementation checklist

  1. Fixed commitments are locked.
  2. Top outcomes receive time before maintenance work.
  3. Every block has a purpose and realistic duration.
  4. Buffers exist between major context switches.
  5. Overflow work is explicitly deferred rather than hidden.

Operating protocol

  1. Enter fixed meetings and personal commitments first.
  2. Rank three weekly outcomes before adding task-level work.
  3. Estimate task duration and identify high-energy versus low-energy work.
  4. Place deep work, admin batches, buffers, and recovery space into real blocks.
  5. Run a capacity check and defer anything that does not fit.
