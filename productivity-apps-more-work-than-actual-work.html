The 4 Signs of Productivity Overhead

A productivity system is failing when its maintenance cost exceeds the clarity or execution it creates.

  • You reorganize tasks more often than you complete them.
  • The system requires duplicate entry across multiple tools.
  • Every missed day creates cleanup work or a reset.
  • The app stores tasks but does not help decide what matters now.
