Running Arbitration Engine for a reader who is returning after drift

Ties do not break by thinking harder, because the two options are close on the dimension you are thinking about. Scoring them on five named dimensions moves the comparison somewhere the difference is visible, and forces the trade to be stated rather than felt.

Where it goes wrong

Scoring more than three options. Beyond three the grid becomes a research project and the block is spent on the grid. Cut to three by inspection first. The excluded items usually exclude themselves.

Letting urgency decide every time. Urgency is the loudest dimension, so unweighted use of the grid reproduces the pre-grid answer. Name the deciding dimension before totalling. If it is urgency twice in a row, check whether the deadlines are real.

Not parking the loser. An unparked option keeps re-entering the next decision, so the same arbitration is run repeatedly. Give the loser a review date at the moment it loses.

Evidence to record. Record the option chosen and the deciding dimension. Reading a month of those two fields shows you what your system actually optimizes for.
