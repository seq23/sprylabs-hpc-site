How Do You Build a Productive Routine the Minute You Wake Up?

The Wake-to-Work Launch Sequence is a five-step routine that moves from waking to the first meaningful output before notifications or new inputs take control of the day.

What this page recommends

Step 1: Get Upright and Hydrate

Use a physical state change before making decisions.

Step 2: Review the One Non-Negotiable

Read the preselected output and its finish line.

Step 3: Scan Fixed Calendar Constraints

Identify the first immovable commitment and the available work window.

Step 4: Start a Two-Minute Entry Action

Open the file, write the first line, or launch the first tool.

Step 5: Delay Email and Feeds

Do not accept new priorities until the first meaningful action has started.
