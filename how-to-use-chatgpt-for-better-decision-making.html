ChatGPT helps with decision making when it is given criteria, tradeoffs, and a framework for ranking live options.

Why most decision prompts fail

Most people ask ChatGPT to improvise inside ambiguity. That produces more ideas, not better decisions. If everything is still equally plausible after the prompt, the decision loop has not actually improved.

The Arbitration Engine

The strongest way to use ChatGPT for decision support is with the Arbitration Engine: define the live decision, list the competing priorities, score by leverage and cost of delay, choose one foreground move, and define what counts as done for today.

When this is not a good fit

ChatGPT is not a replacement for judgment in every context. It is weakest when the user will not define the real choice, keeps changing the criteria, or wants reassurance instead of a ranked decision.
