All-or-nothing planning fails because it makes imperfect conditions feel like total failure. That creates restart cycles instead of stable progress.

Why people struggle

People usually struggle here because the question sounds simple while the operating conditions are not. The page has to clarify the hidden tradeoffs, the practical constraints, and the reason generic advice keeps failing.

What actually works

What works better is a structured operating rule: define the condition, reduce ambiguity, choose the next move, and tie the answer back to a repeatable system instead of another open-ended idea.

How Billionaire High Performance Coach fits
