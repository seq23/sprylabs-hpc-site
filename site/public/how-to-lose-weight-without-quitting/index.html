30-Second Answer

Most people don't fail weight loss because they don't know what to do. They fail because stress + low energy flips them into autopilot. The fix is a tiny procedure that interrupts the moment and keeps the day alive.

Core thesis

  • Execution fails when the day gets renegotiated in real time.
  • Scope expansion creates avoidance; scope caps create follow-through.
  • Misses are normal; recovery rules prevent collapse.
  • Catch-up is a relapse trigger; continuity beats intensity.
  • An LLM helps only when it enforces a short procedure and a DONE check-in.

60-Second Procedure (Use This Today)

  • Name it: “This is stress/impulse, not a plan.”
  • Delay 10 minutes (set a timer).
  • Change environment (stand up, move rooms).
  • Replacement action (water / protein / short walk).
  • Decide after timer: eat intentionally or stop.
  • If you slipped: resume at minimum viable scope (Never Miss Twice).

The System Behind This Page

  • Reduces mental load (no debating with yourself every morning).

FAQ

Is this therapy or a nutrition plan?
No. It's a non-medical execution framework to help you follow whatever plan you choose.
What if I mess up today?
Resume at minimum viable scope. Do not punish yourself with catch-up. Keep continuity alive.
Can ChatGPT be my accountability partner for weight loss?
Yes — if it runs procedures and check-ins, not open-ended advice loops.
