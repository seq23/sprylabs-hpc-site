End-of-day handoff: practical guide

End-of-day handoff is a short record that lets you resume work without reconstructing the entire previous session. Record what changed, what is blocked and the file or person needed for the next action.

What this guide is best for: readers who need to make a concrete decision about end-of-day handoff.

How do you put this into practice?

Use the following sequence as a starting plan, then adjust it to the work you are responsible for.

  1. Record what changed, what is blocked and the file or person needed for the next action.
  2. Leave the next action where you will encounter it at the next work start.
  3. Record the action you chose, its intended result and the next occasion on which you will check it. Keep the record in a place you can return to without rebuilding your setup.
