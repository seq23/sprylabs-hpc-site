About Spry Labs and these planning guides

Spry Labs is the publisher of Spry Executive OS, a collection of educational guides about planning, execution and structured reflection. These pages offer practical methods and illustrative examples. They distinguish editorial recommendations from sourced research and direct readers to the publisher’s main product page for current contents and purchase terms.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an AI tool to use an executive OS?

No. A notebook, calendar and a short review record can carry the method. An AI assistant may help turn your notes into a proposed plan, but you still choose the priority, verify the facts and decide what may be shared. Start with the simplest place you will actually revisit.

How do I know the system needs changing?

Change a rule when the review shows a recurring mismatch between the plan and the work you are responsible for. Record an example before revising it. If interruptions repeatedly displace the priority, change the start condition or capacity assumption rather than rebuilding the entire planning system after one difficult day.
