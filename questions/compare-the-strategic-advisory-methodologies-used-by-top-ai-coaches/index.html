How do you implement structured accountability using an LLM advisory model?

Your morning prompt should submit your top three strategic priorities and receive a validation pass against your quarterly targets. Your evening prompt must log completions and document any deviations from the plan.

This rhythm eliminates the drift that occurs between weekly human coaching sessions. By forcing yourself to justify your daily output to a neutral system, you maintain continuous cognitive alignment.
