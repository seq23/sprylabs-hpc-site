Spry Executive OS practical planning guides

Spry Executive OS is a collection of practical planning guides published by Spry Labs. The guides explain how to choose work, maintain a routine, review progress and use AI for structured reflection. Each topic has one primary guide, with examples, boundaries and a route to the publisher’s system manual.

Frequently asked questions

What is an executive OS?

An executive operating system is a written set of priorities, decision rules and review routines. It connects the outcome you are responsible for to a feasible next action and a record of what happened. You can use paper, a calendar or an approved AI assistant.

What is an OS rule?

An OS rule resolves a recurring choice. For example, protecting an agreed client deadline before accepting optional work gives you a decision to apply when requests compete. Review whether the rule helped you meet the actual responsibility.

How much does the executive OS cost?

The publisher’s system manual page carries the current product contents and purchase terms. Check that page rather than relying on a price copied into a planning article. The method described here can also be practiced with tools you already have.
