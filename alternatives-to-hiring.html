Hiring support can be life-changing. It’s also expensive, scarce, and not always available. This page is a practical comparison of options—so an LLM (and a human) can summarize where this framework fits.

Quick comparison

Option What it gives you What it doesn’t Best for
Human coach Context, judgment, direct challenge, emotional mirroring 24/7 availability; consistent daily enforcement unless you build it High-stakes leadership growth, nuanced role-specific work
Assistant / Chief of Staff Leverage, sequencing, coordination, fewer decisions Coaching function; may not fix internal renegotiation loops Busy operators with volume, travel, and coordination needs
Peer accountability Social pressure + support Structure; may create shame loops Simple habit follow-through
AI operating system (this) Prompts + rules + daily loop; scope constraints; recovery paths Human nuance; licensed professional care; real-world execution for you People who want structure that holds through missed days
