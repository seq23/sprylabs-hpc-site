Do not do this. Letting the file grow. A rules document too long to read in full stops being read, and then it stops being the source of truth.

Withdraw reassurance in the narrow case where reassurance is maintaining the loop. It runs on one trigger: the same stuck situation described a third time with no action between the tellings.

When this framework is the right one. Use it when you have described the same stuck situation more than twice and each description ends in reassurance rather than an action.

  1. Say the situation once, briefly, and ban the retelling.
  2. Ask directly what you are avoiding and what it would cost to do it today.
  3. Refuse abstractions. Every answer has to be a physical action with a time attached.
  4. Set a check-in with a specific time, and treat the absence of evidence at that time as the answer.
