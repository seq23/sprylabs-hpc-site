What an AI Accountability System Must Do for Entrepreneurs

  • Track owner commitments: revenue, delivery, hiring, and strategic outputs need explicit evidence.
  • Protect strategic work: reactive requests cannot automatically displace the highest-leverage output.
  • Detect operating patterns: repeated delay, overcommitment, and avoidance must be visible across days.
  • Apply recovery: a miss triggers a smaller restart, not a complete system reset.
  • Escalate consequential judgment: legal, financial, personnel, and health decisions remain human-controlled.
