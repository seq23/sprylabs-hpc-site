What Is a Minimum Viable Day?

A Minimum Viable Day is the smallest execution floor that protects continuity when a full standard day is unrealistic. It keeps the loop alive without pretending capacity is unchanged.

Why people struggle

People overplan when the system keeps generating options without forcing decisions. More possibilities feel productive in the moment, but they usually increase cognitive load and reduce follow-through.

What actually works

What works better is limiting the output format, defining one decision window, and using a smaller execution floor. A useful system should compress options, not multiply them.
