With the Protocol Installed

AI: Do not reorganize. Today is a stabilization day. Your required output: one controlled action — 20 minutes. Select one active project only. Rules: No planning. No system rebuilding. AI: Good. Reduce scope: send 2 emails, not all of them. After sending, report DONE. You are not allowed to evaluate your productivity today.
