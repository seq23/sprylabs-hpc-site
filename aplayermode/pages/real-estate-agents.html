An AI assistant for real estate agents

A real estate assistant handles the admin around an agent's deals: follow-ups, scheduling, paperwork chasing and keeping in touch with past clients. A Player Mode does the planning and follow-up part for the agent, as an app.

Quick answer

Before you hire a virtual assistant, list the tasks you would hand over. Many are follow-ups and scheduling, which an app can carry: A Player Mode plans your day, catches promises you made in email, and on Executive Suite tracks birthdays and relationships. Showings, calls and paperwork still need you or a person.

Best for: Solo agents and small teams who are not ready to hire, or who want the follow-ups kept before they do.

What A Player Mode does for an agent

  • Every morning: your #1 move and the order of the day, around showings you already have.
  • Follow-ups: promises in your mail ("I'll send comps tomorrow") are caught before they slip.
  • Relationships: on Executive Suite, the people who matter and the dates to reach out.
  • Bad weeks: a deal falls through and the plan shrinks instead of stacking up.

Mail: mail features (the Radar, reply drafts and bill flags) work with Outlook mail today; Gmail is planned. If your mail is Gmail, the planning, coaching and calendar parts work now. Calendars connect from Google or Outlook. Keep client and transaction documents in your brokerage's systems.

Hire a VA or start with an app?

What it never does

  • Never spends your money. Autopilot can cancel a subscription you name. It never buys anything for you, upgrades a plan or enters a card.
  • Never sends on its own. Each message waits for your tap, or for a rule you set. Rules expire, and you can switch one off any time.
  • Undo. Autopilot’s calendar changes and drafts undo in one tap. A send can’t be undone, and Autopilot says so before you allow it.
  • Plain billing. No free trial to forget about. Cancel in the app or your store account, no call needed. On a yearly plan, we email you at least 15 days before it renews.

Frequently asked questions

What does a real estate assistant do?

Admin around the agent's deals: scheduling, follow-ups, paperwork chasing, listing prep and staying in touch with past clients. Some are licensed and can do more; check your state's rules.

Is there an AI assistant for real estate agents?

Yes. A Player Mode plans an agent's day, catches the follow-ups they promised and tracks relationships. It is not a CRM or an MLS tool, and it does not make calls.

Does A Player Mode replace my CRM?

No. Keep your CRM for leads and transactions. A Player Mode runs your day and your follow-ups around it.

Does it work with Gmail?

Calendar and planning, yes. Mail features (the Radar, reply drafts and bill flags) work with Outlook today; Gmail is planned.
