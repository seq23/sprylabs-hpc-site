Does A Player Mode write my training or give medical advice?

No: it plans when your training happens and helps you follow through; your coach decides what the training is.

Tell it your sessions, your event date and your week, and it builds the plan, puts rest days in it and reminds you. It is not a coach's program, it does not track your body, and it does not give medical or psychological advice. For any of that, ask your coach, trainer or a licensed professional.

What does the Mindset Coach do on a hard day?

It gets you back to one small action after a miss, instead of a whole new plan.

The Mindset Coach is one of your five roles. When a day goes wrong, it stops the "I blew it" spiral and gives you one next step. The Accountability Partner holds the line between days, so a missed session turns into a smaller day tomorrow, not a restart. A Player Mode is not therapy and is not a substitute for a licensed professional.

Frequently asked questions

Does it replace my coach?

No. Your coach or trainer decides the training. A Player Mode plans the week around it: when each session fits, the rest days, school or work, and the things you said you would do. Many athletes use it alongside a coach so the plan survives a busy week.

Can it plan training around school or a job?

Yes. When you set it up you can pick more than one area of your life, for example Athlete / training plus Student or a job. Each keeps its own lane, and your morning plan puts one priority first so the day still fits.
