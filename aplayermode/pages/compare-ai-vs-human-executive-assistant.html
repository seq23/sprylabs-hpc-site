AI vs human executive assistant: what each costs, and what each can do

A human executive assistant is a person who runs your calendar, inbox and errands. A virtual assistant service rents you part of one person's week. An AI executive assistant is software that does the routine part of the job (the agenda, the follow-ups, the calendar fixes) for a monthly app price.

Quick answer

A salaried executive assistant earns a US median of $76,590 a year before benefits. A dedicated virtual assistant service starts around $1,500 to $1,600 a month for 55 hours. An AI executive assistant like A Player Mode is $24.99 to $79.99 a month. The person brings judgement, phone calls and errands; the software brings every morning, every weekend and a perfect memory for what you promised.

Best for: Founders, executives and small business owners deciding whether to hire, outsource or start with software.

What can a human assistant do that AI can't?

Anything that needs a body, a voice or a relationship.

  • Make and take phone calls on your behalf.
  • Run errands, sign for packages, deal with a building or a vendor in person.
  • Read a room: know which investor needs a call instead of an email.
  • Make judgement calls you never wrote down.

If most of what you need is on that list, hire a person. Software won't replace them.

What does an AI executive assistant do better?

Consistency, memory and price.

  • Your agenda is ready every morning, including Saturday.
  • The Radar catches promises you made in email ("I'll send it Thursday") before they are due.
  • After a bad day it shrinks tomorrow instead of piling up catch-up.
  • It costs less a month than two hours of a human assistant.

Mail features (the Radar, reply drafts and bill flags) work with Outlook mail today; Gmail is planned. Calendars connect from Google or Outlook.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a virtual executive assistant cost?

Dedicated services such as Prialto list $1,500 to $1,600 a month for a 55-hour unit, with a 90-day minimum (prialto.com/pricing, 21 Nov 2026). Freelance assistants vary widely by country and experience. An AI executive assistant app costs tens of dollars a month.

What is an AI secretary?

Another name for an AI executive assistant: software that keeps your calendar, inbox follow-ups and daily agenda in order. It does not answer your phone unless it is a call-answering product, which A Player Mode is not.

Can I use both?

Yes, and many people with a human assistant should. The AI keeps the plan and the follow-ups every day; the person handles calls, errands and anything that needs judgement.

What is the best AI executive assistant?

The one that keeps working when you stop paying attention. Look for three things: it has your day ready without being asked, it remembers what you promised, and it never acts or spends without your say-so.
