A Player Mode disclaimer

A Player Mode is a planning and coaching app made with AI, from Spry Labs. It is not medical, mental-health, financial or legal advice. It promises no income and no results. No billionaire, celebrity or other real person endorses it or takes part in it. Every price is shown with how it renews and how to cancel.

This page covers the A Player Mode app and the Billionaire High Performance Coach OS digital product. The full Terms of Service and Privacy Policy are on app.aplayermode.com.

Does A Player Mode promise income or results?

No. It helps you plan, follow through and recover from bad days. What happens next depends on you.

What we do

  • Show what the app does with plain examples, such as a follow-up caught before it slips. Examples show how it works, not what you will get.
  • Answer "Will it make me rich?" with no.

What we never do

  • Promise income, earnings, a raise, a business result, weight loss or any other outcome.
  • Show income figures, "results guaranteed" or before-and-after stories.

Is it medical, mental-health, financial or legal advice?

No. It is a tool for organising your days. It does not replace a doctor, therapist, financial adviser or lawyer.

What we do

  • Say "not medical advice" wherever the app plans movement, food or sleep, and keep any health goal at a safe pace, with a nudge to see a clinician.
  • Ask for your separate consent before the app stores any health answer (see the Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy linked from the Privacy Policy).
  • Give a crisis line when someone says they may be in danger. In the US, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline); elsewhere, call your local emergency number.

What we never do

  • Diagnose, treat or claim to treat any condition, including depression, anxiety or ADHD.
  • Present the Cognitive Behavioral Mindset Coach as therapy. It is a role name for a coach that interrupts a spiral and hands you one small next step; it is not cognitive behavioural therapy.
  • Recommend investments, financial products or a diet.

Who are the five advisers?

Software, not people. The Executive Coach, Executive Assistant, Chief of Staff, Accountability Partner and Cognitive Behavioral Mindset Coach are five roles the app plays with AI.

What we do

  • Say that the app is made with AI, and that AI can be wrong, so you check anything important.
  • Show you every draft email or calendar change before it goes out, unless you turned on Autopilot for that kind of task, and log everything Autopilot does.

What we never do

  • Pretend a human is reading or replying.
  • Let the app spend your money. Autopilot can cancel a subscription you set a rule for; it never buys, pays or transfers.

No. "Billionaire" in Billionaire High Performance Coach, and "the team billionaires keep", describe a kind of support, not a person.

What we do

  • Describe the support stack people at the top often use (a coach, an assistant, a chief of staff) in general terms.

What we never do

Yes, when there are any. We only show quotes from real users who gave written permission.

What we do
