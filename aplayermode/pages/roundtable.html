The kind of team billionaires keep is simple: people who plan their day, remember what they promised and keep them steady. A Player Mode gives you your own executive roundtable of five roles in one app, from $9.99/month for the first 100 members.

Who sits at your roundtable?

Five advisers, one table: each takes one job off your head.

What does each one do for your day?

Each role does one job every day, so you stop doing all five alone.

At your tableWhat it does for your day
Executive CoachCalm, clear direction under pressure. You stop talking yourself in circles. Before a hard call or a big decision, it asks one question at a time until you know your next move.
Executive AssistantSequences the day and tells you exactly where to start. You stop deciding what is first. Your agenda arrives each morning with the one priority on top, sized to the time you really have.
Chief of StaffKeeps projects, priorities and long-term goals lined up. You stop holding the whole map. The Radar catches the follow-up you promised by Thursday and the renewal due next week.
Accountability PartnerHolds the line between days when motivation disappears. You stop restarting. Miss a day and the next one shrinks to a minimum day: Never Miss Twice, no catch-up.
Cognitive Behavioral Mindset CoachInterrupts the spiral after a miss and gets you back to one small action. You stop the shame loop. When your head is full it switches to Recovery and gives you one small action, not a new plan.

Why do people at the top keep a team like this?

Because structure lasts longer than motivation: a team holds the plan when a person runs out of energy.

They keep a coach for clarity, someone to sequence the day, someone to keep the projects lined up and someone to hold them to what they said. Most people are expected to do all of that by themselves. Frequently asked questions

Is this a course or a coaching service?

No. A Player Mode is an app. Its five roles plan your day, keep track of what you promised and coach you through hard moments. It is not a course, not therapy and not a human coaching service.

Do I talk to five different people?

No. The five roles work as one team inside one app. You see one plan for the day, one Radar of what is about to slip and one coach when you are stuck; each role does its part behind that.

Will it make me rich?

No, and no app can promise that. A Player Mode helps you plan, follow through and recover from bad days. What you do with that time is yours. Results are examples, not guarantees.
