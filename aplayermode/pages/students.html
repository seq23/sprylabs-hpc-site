For students: classes, deadlines and the rest of your life, planned

A student planner keeps classes, deadlines and the rest of your life in one place and turns big work into small steps. A Player Mode does that for college and graduate students: every deadline in one morning plan, exam week broken into steps, and no catch-up guilt after a missed day.

Quick answer

For students, every class has its own deadlines and nothing tells you where to start. A Player Mode puts every deadline in one morning plan, breaks exam week into steps, and shrinks the next day after a miss, from $9.99/month for the first 100 members.

Best for: College, graduate and adult students, 17 and over, balancing classes with a job, family or a thesis.

What overwhelms students, and what comes off your plate?

The overload is every deadline at once; the fix is one plan that says what comes first.

Does A Player Mode teach the material or do the work?

No: it plans the work and keeps you on it; your classes teach the material.

It does not tutor, write assignments or check answers. It lists what is due, breaks each big piece into steps, books the study blocks and reminds you of the next one. The work, and how it turns out, stays yours.

What happens when a busy week goes wrong?

The plan shrinks instead of piling up: after a miss you get a minimum day, not a catch-up list.

The Accountability Partner works across days with one rule, Never Miss Twice. Miss a day and tomorrow becomes a short floor, like a 15-minute review of the next topic. The Mindset Coach steps in when your head is full and gives you one small action, not a new plan.

Frequently asked questions

Who is it for?

College, graduate and adult students who are 17 or older: people with classes, deadlines and a life around them, such as a job, family or a thesis. It is not built for younger students.

Can it plan classes around a job?

Yes. When you set it up you can pick more than one area of your life, for example Student / studying plus a job. Each keeps its own lane, and your morning plan puts one priority first so the day still fits.
