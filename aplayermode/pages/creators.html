Does it work alongside a day job?

Yes: it puts real work first and keeps each project in its own lane.

Frequently asked questions

How does it turn a goal into steps?

You pick a goal in setup, such as shipping a release, posting on a schedule or finishing a draft. It turns that into a plan and three first steps, like writing the release checklist or batching post ideas, then puts one step into each day.

Is it a scheduling or posting tool?

No. It does not post for you or connect to your social accounts. It plans when the work happens, keeps track of who you owe a reply and coaches you one question at a time when you are stuck.
