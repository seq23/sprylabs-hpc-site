An executive coach when no employer is paying

An executive coach helps a leader get clear on what matters, act on it and keep going through hard stretches. Most executive coaching is bought by companies for their staff. A Player Mode is an AI coach for one person, tied to your real calendar and plan, at an app price.

Quick answer

If you are a founder, freelancer or between employers, you pay for coaching yourself. A Player Mode gives you an AI Executive Coach and Accountability Partner every morning, from $24.99 a month. It is an AI, not a credentialed human coach, and many people use it between sessions with one.

Best for: Founders, independent professionals and anyone whose coaching used to come with a job.

What does an executive coach do?

Clarity, action and follow-up.

  • Helps you choose the few things that matter this quarter.
  • Turns them into what you will do this week.
  • Checks back, so a plan doesn't quietly die.
  • Gets you through a hard stretch without dropping everything.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get an executive coach without my company paying?

Yes. You can hire an independent coach directly, or use an AI coach such as A Player Mode for the daily part at an app price. Many people do both.

Is an AI coach as good as a human executive coach?

It is different. A human brings judgement and experience in scheduled sessions. An AI coach is there every day, knows your plan and calendar, and costs far less. It does not replace a person's judgement.

What is a CEO coach?

An executive coach who works with chief executives and founders on priorities, decisions and leadership. A Player Mode's Executive Coach role does the daily part of that for one person: what matters today, and what to drop.

Is A Player Mode a life coach?

It covers work and life together (Mind, Body and Spirit by default), so it plays some of that role. It is software, not a credentialed coach, and not therapy.
