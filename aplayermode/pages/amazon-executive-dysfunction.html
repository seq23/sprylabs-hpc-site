What is the free next step?

Use the 5-role reset card below each morning, or any time the day falls apart. Answer each line on paper in a few words. Print this page or keep it open on your phone; nothing to sign up for.

  1. Executive Assistant: what is the first physical step?Name a movement a camera could record, such as “open the file” or “put the shoes by the door”, not a topic.
  2. Chief of Staff: what is the one priority today?One thing that, if done, makes today count. Everything else waits behind it.
  3. Executive Coach: what is the directive when pressure hits?Write one short instruction you will follow when you feel stuck, for example “ten minutes on the first step, then decide”.
  4. Accountability Partner: did yesterday slip?If it did, today is not a restart. Never miss twice: pick the minimum version of today that still counts, and do that.
  5. Mindset Coach: what thought is in the way?Write it down, then rewrite it as the next action. “I’m so behind” becomes “send the one email that is overdue”.
