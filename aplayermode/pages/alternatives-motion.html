A Motion alternative for one person

Motion is an AI calendar and project tool priced per seat for teams. A Player Mode is a personal app for one person: it plans the day, catches the follow-ups you owe and coaches you, across work and home.

Quick answer

If you use Motion on your own and mostly want your day planned and your follow-ups kept, a one-person app may fit better. Motion's pricing page lists Pro AI at $19 and Business AI at $29 per seat a month, each with a monthly allowance of AI credits. A Player Mode is one flat price per person, from $24.99 a month, billed by Apple, Google Play or card, with no credits to count.

Best for: People using Motion alone, or thinking about it, who want a plan for their whole day rather than a team project board.

How is Motion priced?

Per seat, with AI credits.

As of 21 Nov 2026, Motion's pricing page lists two plans: Pro AI at $19 per seat a month with 7,500 credits per seat, and Business AI at $29 per seat a month with 15,000 credits per seat, with a discount for paying yearly. Check the page for today's prices and what a credit covers.

What A Player Mode does each day

  • Morning: your #1 move, the run of the day and the calendar blocks, ready before you ask.
  • During the day: the Radar flags the replies you owe and the promises you made.
  • On Autopilot: it moves flexible meetings and books time inside rules you set, and every change has Undo.
  • After a miss: Never Miss Twice: tomorrow gets smaller, not heavier.

What it never does

  • Never spends your money. Autopilot can cancel a subscription you name. It never buys anything for you, upgrades a plan or enters a card.
  • Never sends on its own. Each message waits for your tap, or for a rule you set. Rules expire, and you can switch one off any time.
  • Undo. Calendar changes and drafts undo in one tap. A send can’t be recalled, so it says so before it goes.
  • Plain billing. No free trial to forget about. Cancel in the app or your store account, no call needed. On a yearly plan, we email you at least 15 days before it renews.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a Motion alternative for personal use?

Yes. A Player Mode is priced and built for one person, with work and personal life in one plan. Motion is built and priced per seat for teams.

Does A Player Mode auto-schedule my calendar?

On Autopilot, yes, inside rules you set: it can add blocks, move flexible meetings and decline invites that break your boundaries. It never moves Deep Work blocks, and calendar changes undo in one tap.

Can I move my tasks from Motion?

There is no import. A Player Mode starts from a few easy taps about your goals and week, then builds the plan with you. Most people re-enter only what matters this week.

Is there a free trial?

No. There is no free trial, so nothing converts to a charge without you choosing it. The Founding 100 price is $9.99 a month, and you can cancel any time.
