What happened to Clockwise?

Clockwise's team joined Salesforce and the product closed.

Clockwise's own site says the product is no longer available from 27 March 2026, that customer data is not transferred to Salesforce, and that it is deleted after access ends. If you relied on it for focus time and meeting moves, you need a new home for that job.

What A Player Mode adds

  • The plan is built for you: your #1 move and the run of the day are ready each morning.
  • Follow-through across days: the Radar catches promises in your mail; Never Miss Twice keeps one bad day from becoming a bad week.
  • Coaching: five modes, from Deep Work to Recovery.
  • Autopilot books and moves time inside your rules, across work and personal calendars, with Undo.

What it never does

  • Never spends your money. Autopilot can cancel a subscription you name. It never buys anything for you, upgrades a plan or enters a card.
  • Never sends on its own. Each message waits for your tap, or for a rule you set. Rules expire, and you can switch one off any time.
  • Undo. Autopilot’s calendar changes and drafts undo in one tap. A send can’t be undone, and Autopilot says so before you allow it.
  • Plain billing. No free trial to forget about. Cancel in the app or your store account, no call needed. On a yearly plan, we email you at least 15 days before it renews.

Frequently asked questions

Is Clockwise shutting down?

It already has. Clockwise's site says the product stopped being available on 27 March 2026 after its team joined Salesforce, and that customer data is deleted rather than transferred.

What is the best Sunsama alternative?

It depends on why you are leaving. If you like planning by hand, another hand planner fits. If the morning planning ritual is the part you want gone, pick an app that builds the plan for you, such as A Player Mode.

What is a good Akiflow alternative?

If you use Akiflow to pull tasks from many inboxes into one calendar, look for that. If what you need is the plan built and the follow-ups kept, A Player Mode does that for one person, work and home together.

Does A Player Mode replace Reclaim?

For one person, largely: on Autopilot it books routines and focus time and moves flexible meetings inside your rules. Reclaim offers team scheduling features that A Player Mode does not.
