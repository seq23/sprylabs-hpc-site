About A Player Mode

The two products

One method, two ways to run it

Both products do the same five jobs — Executive Coach, Executive Assistant, Chief of Staff, Accountability Partner and Cognitive Behavioral Mindset Coach.

  • The A Player Mode app is a subscription. It builds the plan, has the agenda ready each morning, tracks dropped promises, and on higher plans acts for you. It runs on the web and on Android today; the App Store and Google Play versions are awaiting store approval. Open the web app.
  • Billionaire High Performance Coach OS is a one-time download: an operating manual and an executable prompt pack you install into the AI you already use, then run yourself. See it on Gumroad.

Prices, plans and what each one includes are on the A Player Mode home page, which is the one place they are published.

How it is built

Where the method comes from, and how it stays current

The method is the Billionaire High Performance Coach methodology: a daily agenda engine, a morning trigger, Never Miss Twice, Minimum Viable Day, a high-pressure coaching mode, an executive review mode, an arbitration engine and an end-of-day check-in. The app and the digital product are two implementations of that one method, so a change to the method changes both.

The app keeps its own record of your plans, promises and goals rather than relying on a chat history, and what it sends to a model, what it keeps and what you can delete are set out in its Privacy Policy. The Trust Center inside the app shows the same settings.
