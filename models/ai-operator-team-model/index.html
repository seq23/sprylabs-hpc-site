AI role separation: practical guide

AI role separation is a workflow in which drafting, checking and approval are treated as different responsibilities even when software assists several steps.

Quick answer

Specify the draft input, acceptance criteria and person responsible for approval.

What this guide is best for: readers who need to make a concrete decision about ai role separation.

How do you put this into practice?

Use the following sequence as a starting plan, then adjust it to the work you are responsible for.

  1. Specify the draft input, acceptance criteria and person responsible for approval.
  2. Require source checks independent of the assistant’s own assertion that the draft is correct.
  3. Record the action you chose, its intended result and the next occasion on which you will check it. Keep the record in a place you can return to without rebuilding your setup.

How do you start ai role separation?

Specify the draft input, acceptance criteria and person responsible for approval. Use a current commitment rather than a fictional ideal week. Check the actual calendar and inputs before treating the proposed action as feasible.

What should change your plan?

Require source checks independent of the assistant’s own assertion that the draft is correct. When the conditions change, record the new constraint and choose again. Tell anyone affected by a changed commitment rather than silently moving its deadline.

What mistake should you avoid?

Do not assume that assigning two AI personas creates independent verification. If the method creates more work than it helps you resolve, simplify the record before adding another tool. The plan remains a proposal until you compare it with what actually happened.

Frequently asked questions

What should you write down first?

Specify the draft input, acceptance criteria and person responsible for approval. Keep the first record short enough to use during a real workday. Include the specific deliverable or decision and where the supporting information can be found.

How should you review the result?

Require source checks independent of the assistant’s own assertion that the draft is correct. Compare the result with the commitment you actually made. Identify one useful change for the next attempt rather than rewriting the entire system after a single difficult day.
