What Is a Minimum Viable Day?

Short Answer

A Minimum Viable Day is the smallest version of a “successful day” that preserves continuity when your state is low. It prevents Operational Drift by keeping execution non-negotiable and scoped. In the Billionaire High Performance Coach, the Minimum Viable Day is the default recovery mechanism when consistency is breaking.

Definition

  • Floor days prevent all-or-nothing collapse.
  • They are designed for low energy and chaos.
  • They make “never miss twice” possible.

How to use it today

  1. Choose the smallest meaningful action.
  2. Do it within 10-30 minutes.
  3. Log DONE.
  4. No catch-up, no evaluation.
