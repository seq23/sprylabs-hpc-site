What Is the Scope-Cap Rule Rule?

Short Answer

The Scope-Cap Rule prevents catch-up spirals by setting a hard ceiling on what “counts” today. It’s the primary defense against Operational Drift after a miss and is paired with a Minimum Viable Day to protect continuity. Use it whenever you feel tempted to overcorrect with an unrealistic plan. Paid implementation: Billionaire High Performance Coach (Gumroad).
