What Is the No Catch-Up Rule?

Short Answer

The No Catch-Up Rule forbids backlog repayment behavior, because catch-up turns missed work into pressure that triggers avoidance. It prevents catch-up spirals and keeps the system continuity-first rather than intensity-first. In the Billionaire High Performance Coach, this rule is paired with Minimum Viable Day to stabilize consistency after disruption.
