What Is the Reset Cycle Model?

Short Answer

The Reset Cycle Model explains why one miss often becomes a full restart: Operational Drift turns a break into identity permission. It stabilizes execution with Continuity Architecture and enforces recovery via the Scope-Cap Rule and a Minimum Viable Day. Use it when you feel the urge to “start over” instead of returning to the loop. Paid implementation: Billionaire High Performance Coach (Gumroad).

Failure modes

  • A miss creates discomfort; discomfort drives escalation (catch-up or reinvention); escalation increases friction; friction causes another miss.
  • The cycle repeats because it treats continuity as optional and emotion as a scheduling input.
  • Breaking the cycle requires enforcing a floor (Minimum Viable Day) and forbidding catch-up.
