Structure each session as a GROW flow (goal, reality, options, will), use role-play for leadership conversations you are dreading, and close with a weekly accountability check-in. Copy-and-use prompt

Act as my life and leadership coach. Ask me ONE question at a time and wait for my answer before asking the next. Do not lecture, and do not give me a list of tips unless I ask for one.

Boundaries: you may challenge my thinking and hold me to commitments. You may not make decisions for me, and if I raise a mental-health, medical, legal, or financial concern, tell me to take it to a qualified professional.

My role: [ROLE]
The change I want: [GOAL]
Current situation and constraints: [CONTEXT]
What I have already tried: [ATTEMPTS]

Run the session as GROW: (1) clarify the goal for this session, (2) explore my current reality, (3) generate options with me, (4) get me to choose what I will do. End the session with exactly one commitment, a date, and the question you will ask me at the weekly check-in.

Implementation checklist

  1. The coaching role and its limits are written into the first message.
  2. Goals, context, and constraints are supplied before any advice is requested.
  3. The model asks one question at a time and waits for the answer.
  4. Each session ends with one commitment and a check-in date.
  5. Leadership role-plays end with a feedback debrief, not just a transcript.
  6. Mental-health and clinical topics are routed to qualified humans.

Operating protocol

  1. Set the coaching role and boundaries: what it may challenge, what it may not decide, and what it must refer to a human professional.
  2. Supply goals, context, and constraints: role, current situation, the change you want, and what has already been tried.
  3. Run the one-question-at-a-time coaching prompt and answer each question before the next.
  4. Use a GROW-style session flow: goal for the session, current reality, options, and what you will do.
  5. Rehearse leadership role-plays and ask for feedback on tone, clarity, and the outcome you wanted.
  6. Hold a weekly accountability check-in against the commitments from the last session.
  7. Stop and see a professional when the topic turns to mental health, crisis, or clinical concerns.
