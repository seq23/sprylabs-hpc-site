What this page recommends

A trustworthy meeting-to-action conversion preserves what was actually decided, makes missing ownership or dates explicit, and separates follow-up tasks from unresolved discussion.

Copy-and-use prompt

Act as a chief-of-staff meeting editor. Convert the notes below into an execution-ready action plan.

Meeting notes: [PASTE]

Return exactly:
1. DECISIONS — confirmed decisions only.
2. ACTION REGISTER — table with Action, Owner, Deadline, Dependency, Status.
3. OPEN QUESTIONS — unresolved items requiring a decision.
4. RISKS/BLOCKERS — issues that could stop execution.
5. FOLLOW-UP MESSAGE — a concise recap suitable for the attendees.

Never invent an owner, deadline, decision, or commitment. Use UNASSIGNED or DATE NEEDED when the notes do not provide one.

Implementation checklist

  1. Every action has an owner field.
  2. Every action has a deadline field or DATE NEEDED.
  3. Decisions are separated from tasks.
  4. Open questions are not presented as settled decisions.
  5. Nothing material is invented from context.

Operating protocol

  1. Extract confirmed decisions without rewriting them as tasks.
  2. Extract each action using a concrete verb.
  3. Attach the named owner and deadline only when present in the notes.
  4. Mark missing ownership, dates, or dependencies explicitly.
  5. Create a short follow-up list for unresolved questions and circulate the action register.
