Principles, rules and routines: practical guide

Principles, rules and routines is three different planning layers: values explain why, decision rules choose between options, and routines specify when to act.

Quick answer

Write one principle and translate it into a choice between two competing requests.

What this guide is best for: readers who need to make a concrete decision about principles, rules and routines.

How do you put this into practice?

Use the following sequence as a starting plan, then adjust it to the work you are responsible for.

  1. Write one principle and translate it into a choice between two competing requests.
  2. Revise the routine when the schedule changes; revise a principle only when the underlying responsibility changes.
  3. Record the action you chose, its intended result and the next occasion on which you will check it. Keep the record in a place you can return to without rebuilding your setup.
