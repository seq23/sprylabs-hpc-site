Accountability check-in prompt: practical guide

Accountability check-in prompt is a request that compares your stated commitment with evidence you supply and proposes a next action for your review.

Quick answer

Use this prompt: My commitment was [deliverable] by [date]. Evidence is [record]. Ask what is missing before proposing the next step.

What this guide is best for: readers who need to make a concrete decision about accountability check-in prompt.

How do you put this into practice?

Use the following sequence as a starting plan, then adjust it to the work you are responsible for.

  1. Use this prompt: My commitment was [deliverable] by [date]. Evidence is [record]. Ask what is missing before proposing the next step.
  2. Keep the check-in focused on a specific commitment instead of requesting a personality judgment.
  3. Record the action you chose, its intended result and the next occasion on which you will check it. Keep the record in a place you can return to without rebuilding your setup.
