Working knowledge system: practical guide

Working knowledge system is a reference collection organized around decisions and deliverables rather than the quantity of saved material. Choose a current project and identify the reference needed for its next decision.

What this guide is best for: readers who need to make a concrete decision about working knowledge system.

How do you put this into practice?

Use the following sequence as a starting plan, then adjust it to the work you are responsible for.

  1. Choose a current project and identify the reference needed for its next decision.
  2. Keep a note when it changes an action or preserves a source you will need to verify.
  3. Record the action you chose, its intended result and the next occasion on which you will check it. Keep the record in a place you can return to without rebuilding your setup.
