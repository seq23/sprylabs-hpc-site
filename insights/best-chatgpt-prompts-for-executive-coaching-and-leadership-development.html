Executive coaching prompts are instructions that make ChatGPT or another model behave like a coach: they set the role, give your context, ask one question at a time and end with a written commitment. The strongest prompts specify the session structure - for example the GROW model of goal, reality, options and will - not just the topic.

What makes a good executive coaching prompt?

Four parts: a role (act as an executive coach who asks one question at a time), your context (role, goal, constraint), a structure (GROW or a decision test) and an ending (finish with one commitment and a check-in date).

What are good AI prompts for managers coaching their teams?

Prompts that prepare one-to-ones: given these notes, list three open questions to ask, one piece of recognition and one commitment to follow up. Managers use AI to prepare; the conversation itself stays human.

What is a weekly leadership review prompt?

Review my week as an executive coach: what did I commit to, what happened, what pattern do you see, and what is the one change for next week? Ask one question at a time.

Frequently asked questions

What are the best OpenAI prompts for executives?

Prompts that impose structure: a decision test (list my assumptions and the cheapest way to test each), a meeting-prep brief and a weekly review. Structure matters more than clever wording.

What is a good manager coaching prompt?

Act as a coaching mentor. Help me prepare a one-to-one with this person: their goal, the current reality, their options, and one commitment for them and one for me.

Is there an AI prompt for an executive coach and mentor?

Yes: act as my executive coach and mentor, ask one question at a time about this decision, challenge my assumptions, then end with one commitment and a date to check it.

What AI prompts help executives most?

The daily priority prompt, the meeting-prep prompt and the weekly review prompt. Together they cover planning, preparation and follow-through, which is where most executive time leaks.
