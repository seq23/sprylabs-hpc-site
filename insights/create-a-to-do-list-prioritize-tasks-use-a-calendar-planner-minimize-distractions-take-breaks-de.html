Clean Workday Assembly Loop

The Clean Workday Assembly Loop turns generic productivity advice into one ordered operating sequence: capture, prioritize, schedule, protect, execute, delegate, and recap.

How to apply it

  1. Capture all open tasks in one place.
  2. Choose the one move with the highest downstream value.
  3. Place the top work in a real calendar block.
  4. Remove or distance the main distraction before starting.
  5. Delegate tasks where your judgment is not required.
  6. Recap what moved, what did not, and the next clean step.
