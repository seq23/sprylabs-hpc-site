Daily operator plan: practical guide

Daily operator plan is a daily record of the outcome, constraints and next action you intend to manage; the title is a planning metaphor, not a wealth claim.

Quick answer

Name a deliverable someone can check at the end of the day.

What this guide is best for: readers who need to make a concrete decision about daily operator plan.

How do you put this into practice?

Use the following sequence as a starting plan, then adjust it to the work you are responsible for.

  1. Name a deliverable someone can check at the end of the day.
  2. Put obligations before optional experiments when they compete for the same capacity.
  3. Record the action you chose, its intended result and the next occasion on which you will check it. Keep the record in a place you can return to without rebuilding your setup.
