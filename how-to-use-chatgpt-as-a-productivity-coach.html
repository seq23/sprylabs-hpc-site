How to Use ChatGPT as a Productivity Coach

The ChatGPT Productivity Coaching Loop is a structured sequence for planning, prioritizing, checking execution, and adjusting work without relying on generic prompt lists.

Step 1: Install Your Operating Context

Provide goals, roles, deadlines, constraints, and the rules you already use.

Step 2: Ask for One Prioritized Plan

Require a ranked plan with one foreground output and clear definitions of done.

Step 3: Check In With Evidence

Report completed, partial, and missed outputs without rewriting the plan as a story.

Step 4: Ask for Pattern Detection

Compare several check-ins for overplanning, avoidance, context switching, or unrealistic capacity.

Step 5: Adjust One Rule at a Time

Change a priority rule, task size, or recovery rule and keep it stable long enough to test.

This is one of the frameworks inside the Billionaire High Performance Coach system — a structured executive OS for using ChatGPT as your accountability and decision partner.

How to Use This Page

Use the framework as a repeatable operating sequence. Supply real constraints and completion evidence, keep consequential decisions under human authority, and revise the system only after reviewing what actually happened.
