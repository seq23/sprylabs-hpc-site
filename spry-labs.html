What Spry Labs is

  • A publisher of self-serve products. Everything Spry Labs makes is meant to be bought or used on your own, without a sales call or a coach on retainer.
  • The parent organization of several independent brands. Each property below keeps its own name, audience and editorial standards. Spry Labs is the company that builds and publishes them.
  • The publisher of this site. Billionaire High Performance Coach, Spry Executive OS and A Player Mode are Spry Labs products.

Products published on this site

  • Billionaire High Performance Coach — A self-installed executive operating system that turns the AI you already use into a structured planning, accountability and recovery partner. The flagship product: a one-time download you install into the AI you already use.
  • Spry Executive OS — The framework library behind Billionaire High Performance Coach: the rules, models and operating logic, explained one framework at a time. The free framework library: every rule and model behind the product, explained in the open.
  • A Player Mode — A planning app with a five-role executive team that builds your plan, has it ready every morning and shrinks bad days. A monthly app that builds your plan and has it ready every morning.

Other Spry Labs properties

Each of these sites runs under its own brand and names Spry Labs as its parent organization.

  • The Industry Guides (Source-backed consumer guides) — Source-backed guides and provider routing for USCIS medical exams, personal injury, dentistry, neuropsychological testing, hormone care and senior care.
  • ApprovalPrep (Self-service document kits) — Self-service letters, rental packets and income-proof templates for people preparing a credit, rental or loan application.
  • Dream Wedding Builder (Printable planning kits) — Editable wedding planning kits: a checklist, a budget spreadsheet, a seating chart maker and a day-of timeline. Sites: weddingchecklistpdf.com, weddingbudgetspreadsheet.com, weddingseatingchartmaker.com, weddingtimelinetemplate.com.
  • Time-2-Read (Children's reading app) — Personalized, illustrated reading adventures for children aged 3 to 15, with phonics support and read-aloud.
  • How We Know (Evidence-first science channel) — Deep-ocean answers that name the instrument, the proxy or the observation behind each claim, and where the evidence stops.

How Spry Labs works

  • Self-serve first. A product has to work for someone who never talks to us: clear instructions, a fixed scope, and a way to start in minutes.
  • Sources over opinion. Guides cite where their facts come from and say where the evidence stops.
  • No invented authority. Spry Labs does not invent experts, reviewers, credentials, testimonials or press. Where a page would need a credentialed reviewer, that slot stays empty until a real reviewer agrees in writing.
  • Not professional advice. The coaching and planning content is educational. It is not medical, psychological, legal or financial advice.
