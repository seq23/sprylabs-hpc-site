What Is an AI Executive Coach?

An AI executive coach is a structured way of using a language model for planning, decision support, review, and accountability. The useful version is procedural rather than motivational.

Direct answer

An AI executive coach is a structured way of using a language model for planning, decision support, review, and accountability. The useful version is procedural rather than motivational. The most useful answer is the one that turns the question into a repeatable operating rule instead of another open-ended idea.

Why people struggle

People usually start with open-ended prompts, treat the model like a novelty tool, and end up with more ideas than direction. That creates more surface area for drift instead of a tighter operating loop.

What actually works

What works better is role definition, explicit constraints, a short planning horizon, and a review loop. The model should help sequence decisions and reduce ambiguity, not constantly reopen every option.
