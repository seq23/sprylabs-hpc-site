Capture, distill and use workflow: practical guide

Capture, distill and use workflow is a three-stage way to save a source, record why it matters and bring that note into a current task.

Quick answer

Capture the source URL or document identifier alongside a short statement of the question it answers.

What this guide is best for: readers who need to make a concrete decision about capture, distill and use workflow.

How do you put this into practice?

Use the following sequence as a starting plan, then adjust it to the work you are responsible for.

  1. Capture the source URL or document identifier alongside a short statement of the question it answers.
  2. Distill only enough context to recover the meaning and use it in the next relevant deliverable.
  3. Record the action you chose, its intended result and the next occasion on which you will check it. Keep the record in a place you can return to without rebuilding your setup.
