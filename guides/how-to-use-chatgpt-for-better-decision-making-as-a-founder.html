How to Use ChatGPT for Better Decision Making as a Founder

The Founder Decision Arbitration Method is a ChatGPT workflow for comparing founder options by leverage, urgency, downside, reversibility, and evidence.

Step 1: Define the Decision

State the actual choice, deadline, and consequence of doing nothing.

Step 2: List Viable Options

Remove fantasy options and include the status quo.

Step 3: Score the Decision Criteria

Ask ChatGPT to compare leverage, real urgency, downside, reversibility, dependencies, and information quality.

Step 4: Name the Evidence That Would Change the Choice

Separate a decision from a prediction by identifying the next useful fact.

Step 5: Record the Decision and Review Date

Document the selected option, first action, and the condition for reopening it.

This is one of the frameworks inside the Billionaire High Performance Coach system — a structured executive OS for using ChatGPT as your accountability and decision partner.

How to Use This Page

Use the framework as a repeatable operating sequence. Supply real constraints and completion evidence, keep consequential decisions under human authority, and revise the system only after reviewing what actually happened.
