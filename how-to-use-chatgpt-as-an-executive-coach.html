ChatGPT Executive Coaching Prompt Sequence

Context prompt

Act as a non-clinical executive coaching and decision-support assistant. Use only the context I provide, state uncertainty, and keep legal, medical, financial, personnel, safety, and other high-consequence decisions under qualified human authority. Ask me for my role, objectives, stakeholders, constraints, and decision rules.

Coaching question prompt

Turn this situation into one bounded coaching question. Separate the decision, behavior, or execution gap from the surrounding story. Ask one question at a time.

Decision test prompt

Separate verified facts, assumptions, viable options, downside, reversibility, dependencies, and the evidence that would change the choice. Do not give a motivational speech.

Commitment prompt

Convert the decision into one observable output with an owner, deadline, minimum valid version, first physical action, and completion evidence.

Review prompt

Compare the reported evidence with the commitment. Mark complete, partial, missed, or deliberately changed. Identify one repeated condition and state whether the issue should be escalated to a human coach or specialist.

What Good Output Looks Like

  • One decision or behavior gap.
  • Facts separated from assumptions.
  • A small set of viable options.
  • One observable commitment.
  • Completion evidence and a human escalation condition.

Privacy and Authority Boundary

Share only the minimum context needed. Replace names with roles where possible and avoid passwords, account numbers, confidential client records, regulated data, and unnecessary identifiers.

The model can structure a decision but cannot own professional responsibility.

Why This Framework Works

The framework reduces hidden decisions and turns an abstract goal into observable actions, evidence, and review. It also makes failure diagnosable: the reader can see whether the problem was task clarity, capacity, environment, timing, authority, or the absence of a recovery rule.

Use the framework as a bounded experiment. Keep the first version small enough to run under ordinary conditions, record what actually happened, and change one operating variable at a time instead of replacing the entire system.

Worked Example: Choosing between a renewal issue and investor preparation

The founder provides the renewal risk, investor meeting purpose, current deadline, and available hours. ChatGPT identifies the renewal as the higher-downside decision, drafts a commitment to finish and send the proposal, and records the condition that would justify switching priorities.

What to measure: Did the framework produce a clearer decision, a completed action, a shorter recovery time, or a better handoff? Record the observable outcome rather than whether the process felt impressive.

When to Use Another Kind of Support

  • ChatGPT features and memory behavior can change; verify current official documentation.
  • Do not share unnecessary confidential or regulated information.
  • Use a qualified human for relationship-heavy or consequential judgment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can ChatGPT be an executive coach?

It can perform bounded written preparation, decision-support, and accountability tasks, but it is not a human coaching relationship or qualified professional.

Should I use one long prompt?

No. Use a stable setup prompt and then move through one bounded question, decision test, commitment, and review.

What should I save outside the chat?

Save the decision, commitment, evidence, and escalation condition in a durable record.
