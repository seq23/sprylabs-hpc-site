Editorial Policy

The Billionaire High Performance Coach editorial policy sets out how Spry Labs writes, sources, reviews and corrects the coaching and planning content on this site, and what that content is not.

What this page recommends

The Billionaire High Performance Coach editorial policy sets out how Spry Labs writes, sources, reviews and corrects the coaching and planning content on this site, and what that content is not.

  • Next step: Get Instant Access

The policy in brief

  • Educational, not professional advice. Pages about habits, focus, burnout, ADHD-style routines, money discipline or health habits explain planning and execution methods. They are not medical, psychological, legal or financial advice, and they never replace a qualified professional.
  • Methods, not promises. We describe what a method asks you to do and where it can fail. We do not promise outcomes, income or recovery.
  • No invented authority. No page names an expert, reviewer, credential, client result, testimonial or press mention that does not exist.
  • Corrections are fixed at the source. A reported error is corrected in the page itself, and the page's updated date changes with it.

Who publishes this content

This site is published by Spry Labs, which also publishes the Spry Executive OS framework library and the A Player Mode app. Pages are bylined to the publisher or to S.L. Taylor, the pen name of the system's creator. S.L. Taylor is not a licensed clinician, therapist, attorney or financial adviser, and no page says otherwise.

How pages are written

  • AI-assisted, rule-governed. Pages are drafted with AI assistance inside a publishing system that applies fixed rules: one question per page, a plain one-sentence answer first, then the method, its limits and a next step.
  • Automated checks before anything ships. Every page passes automated checks for broken links, duplicate answers, missing disclaimers and structured data that does not match the visible page.
  • Commercial intent is disclosed. Spry Labs sells the Billionaire High Performance Coach system, and most pages link to it. The product is ours; the links are not paid placements from anyone else.

Sources and evidence

When a page relies on research, a statistic or another person's method, it names the source and links to it where a public copy exists. When the evidence is thin or contested, the page says so. Frameworks that Spry Labs created, such as the Minimum Viable Day or the Scope-Cap Rule, are labelled as our own methods rather than presented as established science.

Health, mental health and money topics

Some pages touch on burnout, ADHD-style difficulties, weight, sleep or money. On those pages the method is framed as a planning aid only. If you are in crisis, have a medical or mental-health condition, or face a legal or financial decision with real consequences, talk to a qualified professional. In the United States you can call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.

Reviews and testimonials

We do not publish invented reviews, ratings or testimonials. Where a review or rating appears, it comes from a real customer and is shown as given. Where a page would need a credentialed reviewer, that slot stays empty until a real reviewer agrees in writing.

Corrections and contact

If something on this site is wrong, out of date or unclear, write to hello@spryexecutiveos.com with the page address. We correct the page itself rather than adding a note elsewhere. See also the citation methodology and the legal page.
